A little candy. A little machinery. Plenty of clever combinations.

Matchworks is a colorful match-3 puzzle game set in a playful mechanical workshop. Match candies, clear rusty parts and frozen grease, and get the machinery moving before your moves run out.

Build your next clever combination

Create fireworks, hammers, and magnets by matching candies. Combine your tools for bigger effects, break through obstacles, and work toward each board’s objectives. Every move counts.

A complete little workshop adventure

  • 20 handcrafted levels, from guided introductions to more demanding puzzles.
  • Rusty screws and nuts, frozen grease, oil, and motors to work around.
  • Special tools, combinations, and an inventory that carries between levels.
  • A level map, 25 achievements, and a reward wheel powered by leftover moves.
  • An interactive tutorial and an illustrated How to Play guide.
  • English and Turkish, with separate music and sound controls.

How to play

Drag between neighboring candies, or click one candy and then its neighbor, to swap them. Match three or more of the same color to clear them. Complete the objectives at the top of the screen before you run out of moves.

Match four in a line to make a firework, make a 2×2 square for a hammer, or match five in a line for a magnet. Swap a tool with an adjacent candy to activate it—or combine two tools and discover what they do together.

Play in your browser

Click Run game to start. Use the fullscreen button if you want a larger view. Your progress and settings are saved in the current browser; they do not transfer automatically to another browser or device.

The demo finishes after level 20. No ads or in-app purchases.
