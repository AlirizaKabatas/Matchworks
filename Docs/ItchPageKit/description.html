A little candy. A little machinery.

Matchworks is a colorful match-3 puzzle game set in a playful mechanical workshop. Match candies, clear rusty parts and frozen grease, and get the machinery moving before your moves run out.

Make every move count

Work through 20 handcrafted levels, from guided introductions to trickier boards. Create fireworks, hammers, and magnets, then combine them to clear obstacles in different ways.

  • Workshop puzzles: rusty screws and nuts, oil, frozen grease, and motors.
  • Special tools: create them through matches, combine them, and earn inventory tools.
  • Persistent progress: unlock the map, collect 25 achievements, and spend leftover moves on the reward wheel.
  • Play your way: English and Turkish, with separate music and sound controls.

How to play

Drag between neighboring candies, or click two neighbors, to swap them. Match three or more of the same color and complete the objectives at the top before you run out of moves.

Match four in a line for a firework, make a 2×2 square for a hammer, or match five in a line for a magnet. Swap a tool with a neighboring candy to activate it. The interactive tutorial and How to Play guide explain the rest.

A small workshop adventure

Play free in your browser; fullscreen is available. Progress is saved in the current browser. The demo finishes after level 20. No ads or in-app purchases.
