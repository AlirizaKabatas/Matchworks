# Matchworks — current scope

A portrait match-3 portfolio demo made with Unity 6000.5.5f1. Final name: Matchworks. Primary distribution: free browser-playable WebGL at https://alikb.itch.io/matchworks . Native Android/iOS store releases are not required for this portfolio delivery.

The player matches candies, creates fireworks/hammers/magnets, clears mechanical obstacles and completes 20 data-defined boards. Ali owns the final level placements and move counts. There are no ads, purchases or real-money systems. The reward wheel spends leftover gameplay moves only.

One gameplay scene loads level JSON. The first ten levels introduce mechanics through optional replayable tutorials. Completing level20 finishes the demo. Local progress includes inventory, achievements, unlocked levels and settings. No cloud synchronization is claimed.

Current art is a colorful 2D toon workshop, with warm wood, gold rims, cream panels and turquoise accents. Assets and code were produced with AI assistance; see CREDITS.md for sources. Player-balance claims are limited to Ali’s actual playtesting; no retention/A-B results have been measured.
